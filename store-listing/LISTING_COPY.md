## Full / Long Description (use on BOTH stores)

```
Telegram Drive — your own private cloud drive, powered by Telegram.

Tired of "storage full" messages? Telegram Drive turns a private Telegram
group that only YOU can see into your personal Google Drive. Your files live
in Telegram — not on our servers (we don't even have servers).

HOW IT WORKS
1. The app guides you step by step to create a free Telegram bot with
   @BotFather — big buttons, copy buttons, nothing confusing. Most people
   finish in 2 minutes, even for the first time.
2. Create a private Telegram group and add your bot. The app finds your
   group automatically.
3. Done! Send anything into your drive from the app.

WHAT YOU CAN STORE
• Photos, videos, documents, APKs, zip files — any file up to 50 MB each
• Notes and links, saved with one tap
• As much as you want — storage is Telegram's, so there is no drive quota
  filling up. It is effectively unlimited for normal use.

DRIVE FEATURES
• Folders to organise your files
• Fast search across everything you saved
• File list and grid views
• Download anything back to your phone in one tap
• Delete a file in the app and it is also removed from your Telegram group

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your bot token is stored ONLY on your phone.
• Your files go directly from your phone to YOUR private Telegram group,
  using your own bot. The developer never sees, receives, or stores your
  files, your token, or your group.
• No sign-up, no account with us, nothing to hack — your drive is your
  Telegram account.

FREE WITH ADS, PREMIUM ON GOOGLE PLAY
Telegram Drive is free, supported by ads (a small banner and an occasional
full-screen ad after an upload). On the Google Play version, a one-time
$10 lifetime Premium purchase removes all ads forever — it is NOT a
subscription. In this store's version, ads keep the app free and the
Premium purchase button will point you to Google Play.

REQUIREMENTS
• A free Telegram account
• Android 8.0 or higher
• Internet connection for uploading and downloading

Questions or problems? Email us any time: foriadahemed2000@gmail.com
```
