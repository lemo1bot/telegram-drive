# Privacy Policy — Telegram Drive

**Effective date: 27 November 2026**
**App:** Telegram Drive (package `com.foriad.telegramdrive`)
**Developer contact:** foriadahemed2000@gmail.com

This policy is written in simple words, on purpose. It explains exactly what
Telegram Drive does with your information. The short version:

> **We have no servers. We collect nothing. Your files and your Telegram bot
> token never come to us.**

---

## 1. What the app stores — only on your phone

Telegram Drive stores the following **only in the app's private storage on
your own device** (Android SharedPreferences). None of it is sent to the
developer:

- Your **Telegram bot token** (the secret key you paste during setup)
- Your **private group Chat ID**
- A **local index** of the files you saved (file names, sizes, folder names,
  and the Telegram file/message IDs needed to download or delete them)
- Whether **Premium** is active on this device
- Normal app settings (for example, list or grid view)

If you uninstall the app, all of this is deleted from your phone by Android.

## 2. Where your files go — only to your own Telegram group

When you upload a file, photo, note, or link, the app sends it **directly
from your phone to Telegram's servers** (`api.telegram.org`, the Telegram
Bot API), addressed to **your own private Telegram group**, using **your own
bot**. When you download, the file comes back the same way, directly from
Telegram to your phone.

- The developer of Telegram Drive does **not** receive, see, store, or have
  access to your files, your bot token, your Chat ID, or your Telegram group
  at any time. There is no developer server in between — the app talks to
  Telegram only.
- Your files in Telegram are governed by **Telegram's own Privacy Policy**:
  https://telegram.org/privacy
- Your bot token is sent only to `api.telegram.org`, because that is how the
  Telegram Bot API works. Keep your token secret, like a password: anyone
  who has it can control your bot. If you think it leaked, talk to
  @BotFather in Telegram and revoke / regenerate it.

## 3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of Telegram Drive shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**
(banner ads and occasional interstitial ads). To show ads, Google may
collect and use:

- Advertising ID and device information
- Approximate location (country/region level)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected by **Google**, not by the developer, and is handled
under **Google's Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy
and Google's advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID any time in your phone's
Settings → Privacy → Ads. Premium users see no ads, so no ad data is
requested for them inside the app.

## 4. Purchases — Google Play Billing

In the Google Play version of the app, the optional **Premium** one-time
purchase ($10, lifetime, not a subscription) is processed entirely by
**Google Play Billing**. The developer never sees your card number or
payment details. Google handles payments under its own policies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app only records, on your device, whether Premium was purchased, so it
can remove the ads and restore the purchase on a new phone.

## 5. What we do NOT do

- We do **not** collect personal information (no name, email, contacts,
  location, or photos are collected by the developer).
- We do **not** run analytics or tracking of our own.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share any data — we have no data to share.
- The app is **not directed at children** and contains advertisements.

## 6. Permissions

The app uses the Internet permission to talk to Telegram and to load ads.
File access happens only through Android's system file picker — you choose
each file yourself. The app does not scan your phone's storage.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same URL
as the old one, with a new effective date at the top.
